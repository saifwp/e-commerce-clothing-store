## Overview  
Born from the legacy of globally acclaimed fashion house **KAP**, **ARMAS** is a new-age fashion brand designed for the modern woman. With a focus on **original design, quality materials**, and **trend-forward collections**, ARMAS speaks to women who value **femininity, freedom**, and **individual expression**.  

This custom WordPress website captures the essence of the brand with bold visuals, curated collections, and a streamlined shopping experience that works seamlessly across all devices.
